About

About us

Z.R.A.D.A is a coffee and pastry spot in Bitera, Gianyar. Built on slow mornings, good beans, and the belief that everything tastes better when it's made with care.

How it started

Five friends, one kitchen, and too many ideas.

We started Z.R.A.D.A because Gianyar needed a place that felt like home — no pretension, just good coffee and better pastries.

Every bundle on the menu came from a real order we got asked to make at 2AM.

Now we're here to keep it simple, grounded, and delicious.
